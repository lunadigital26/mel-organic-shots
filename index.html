Meet Mel

It started with one tired winter and a cheap juicer.

Hi, I'm Melissa. For years I've pressed shots for my family, my friends, and anyone at work who'd let me hand them one. Ginger for the mornings, turmeric for the achy days, elderberry when everyone around me was sniffling.

People kept asking where they could buy them. So now you can. I still pick the produce, peel the ginger and press every batch myself in Methuen. If I wouldn't give it to my own family, it doesn't go in a bottle.

Questions

Good to know.

How long do the shots last?

They're raw and unpasteurized, so keep them cold and drink within 4 days of pickup. You can freeze them for up to a month; thaw in the fridge overnight.

When should I take a shot?

Most people take one in the morning, before or with breakfast. Ginger Fire and Fire Cider are strong, so start with half if you're new.

Is everything organic?

Yes. Mel buys certified organic produce and raw local honey. The ingredient list on each bottle is the whole recipe.

Can I set up a weekly order?

Yes. Choose the Weekly Ritual bundle and write "repeat weekly" in your order notes. Mel will confirm by text.

I'm pregnant, nursing or on medication. Are these safe?

Ingredients like turmeric, ginger, garlic and elderberry can interact with some medications. Please check with your doctor first. Mel is happy to tell you exactly what's in each shot.
